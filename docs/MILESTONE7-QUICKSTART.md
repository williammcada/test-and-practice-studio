# Cross-course practice quickstart

Open `Test-and-Practice-Studio-v0.29.0-rc.2.html` in your browser. This version contains all six working banks and the cross-course connection explorer.

1. Preview a working question and click **Find related tasks**, or scroll to **Find easier, harder and related tasks** and choose a topic and starting task.
2. Choose the connection type and suggested course. Suggestions show the source course, lesson and reason. Use **Preview** to inspect the actual question before adding it.
3. Click **Add to draft**. To keep the starting task too, click **Add starting task**. Mix entries from any of the six banks; use Remove or Undo to adjust the draft.
4. Generate the manual packet, review student pages and teacher keys, then export Word or use Print / Save as PDF. Download your draft or teacher session to reopen it later.

For the perimeter example, choose topic **Perimeter**, starting task **Perimeter of a rectangle — Course 1 — M7 Perimeter rectangle**, connection **Harder version**, and suggested course **Introduction to PreAlgebra (8/7)**. Preview **Perimeter of an irregular quadrilateral**, then add it. To differentiate downward, use that quadrilateral as the starting task and choose **Easier version**.

Only 8/7 has a confirmed local Grade 5 label. Other courses display school grade unassigned. Task comparisons depend on mathematical demands, not grade labels. The existing GradeCam workflow remains Grade 5 only.

All 2,184 working entries have topic analysis. Reviewed paths include 464 entries; other entries remain discoverable as related content with no unsupported difficulty rating. If a filter returns no reviewed connection, choose Related or explore another starting task. Shared-provider warnings identify repeated coverage without removing your selections. New number variants can still differ in difficulty, so preview the actual task.
