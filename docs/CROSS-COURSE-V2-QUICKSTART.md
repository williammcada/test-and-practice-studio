# Cross-course practice quickstart

Open `Test-and-Practice-Studio-v0.30.0-rc.1.html` in your browser. Course choices now show Intermediate 4 / Grade 3, Course 1 / Grade 4, and 8/7 / Grade 5. Algebra 1/2, Algebra 1 and Algebra 2 show **Tracked · not grade-locked**.

1. Preview a working question and click **Find related tasks**, or choose a topic and starting task in the connection explorer.
2. Choose **Harder version**, **Easier version**, **Prerequisite**, **Extension**, or **Related topic**, then choose the suggested course. Read the demand explanation and preview the actual question.
3. Choose **Add to draft**. Keep the starting task with **Add starting task**, or remove it if you want a replacement. Course filters never change existing selections. Remove and Undo remain available.
4. Build the manual packet, inspect student pages and teacher keys, and export Word or use Print / Save as PDF. Download the draft or teacher session before closing.

For the requested perimeter example: choose **Perimeter of a rectangle — Course 1 · Grade 4**, **Harder version**, and **Introduction to PreAlgebra (8/7) · Grade 5**. Preview **Perimeter of an irregular quadrilateral** and add it. Use the quadrilateral as the starting task and choose **Easier version** to differentiate downward.

All 2,184 working entries have topic links. Reviewed paths cover 1,297 entries; 487 have cross-course easier/harder results and 922 have directional results including prerequisites/extensions. If a filter is empty, explore related tasks or another starting task. Repeated providers and conflicting demand-level evidence never receive an unsupported difficulty label. Always check the actual number variant and required method before assigning it.

Grade labels describe this school's course placement. They do not rank task difficulty or expand GradeCam eligibility: GradeCam remains Grade 5 only. Teacher review is still required for proof, construction, graphing and designated symbolic work.
